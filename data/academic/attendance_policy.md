# Attendance Policy

Students must attend at least **75%** of the scheduled contact hours for each
course. Attendance is recorded by the instructor at every session.

## Consequences of falling below 75%

A student whose attendance in a course falls below 75% is barred from sitting
the final examination for that course and receives a grade of **DN** (denied),
which is calculated as an F for GPA purposes.

The Registrar issues a written warning to the student portal when attendance in
any course reaches 85%, and a second warning at 80%, so that the student has
notice before the threshold is crossed.

## Medical and compassionate exemptions

Absences supported by documentation are excluded from the 75% calculation.
Documentation must be submitted to the Registrar within **5 working days** of
returning to campus. Accepted documentation includes a medical certificate from
a licensed practitioner, a hospital admission record, or a death certificate for
an immediate family member.

Exemption requests submitted more than 5 working days after the absence are
considered only in exceptional circumstances and require the approval of the
College Dean.
