# Transcripts and Enrolment Letters

## Official transcript

An official transcript is a sealed, stamped record of all courses attempted and
grades earned. Requests are made on the student portal under Student Records.

- Processing time: **3 working days**
- Fee: **20 SAR** per copy
- Collection: Registrar's Office, Building 1, or postal delivery for an
  additional 15 SAR

## Unofficial transcript

An unofficial transcript can be downloaded immediately and free of charge from
the student portal. It is not stamped and is not accepted by most employers or
other universities.

## Enrolment letter

A letter confirming current enrolment status, expected graduation date, and
study load. Issued free of charge within **1 working day**. Commonly required
for visa applications, bank accounts, and employer sponsorship.

## Holds

Transcripts are not released to students with an outstanding financial hold,
an unreturned library item, or an incomplete exit clearance. The hold must be
cleared before the request is processed. Holds are visible on the student
portal under Student Records.
