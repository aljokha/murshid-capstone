# Grading Scale and GPA Calculation

Al-Noor University uses a 4.00 point scale. Every course grade converts to grade
points, and the GPA is the credit-weighted average of those points.

| Letter | Percentage | Grade points |
|---|---|---|
| A+ | 95-100 | 4.00 |
| A  | 90-94  | 3.75 |
| B+ | 85-89  | 3.50 |
| B  | 80-84  | 3.00 |
| C+ | 75-79  | 2.50 |
| C  | 70-74  | 2.00 |
| D+ | 65-69  | 1.50 |
| D  | 60-64  | 1.00 |
| F  | below 60 | 0.00 |

The GPA is calculated as the sum of (grade points x credit hours) for all
completed courses, divided by the total credit hours attempted. Courses graded
`W` (withdrawn) carry no grade points and are excluded from the credit hours
attempted. Courses graded `F` are included and count as zero.

A term GPA of 3.75 or above with no grade below B places a student on the
Dean's List for that term.

Grades are published on the student portal within ten working days of the final
examination period. Once published, a grade may only be changed through the
formal grade appeal process.
