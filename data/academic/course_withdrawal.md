# Course Withdrawal

A student may withdraw from an individual course after the add/drop period has
closed. A withdrawn course appears on the transcript with a grade of **W**,
which carries no grade points and does not affect the GPA.

## Deadline

The withdrawal deadline is **the end of week 10** of the term. Withdrawal
requests submitted after the end of week 10 are not accepted, and the student
receives whatever grade they earn in the course.

## Limits

- A student may withdraw from a maximum of **4 courses** over the entire
  degree programme.
- A student may not withdraw below the minimum full-time load of 12 credit
  hours in a term without the written approval of their Academic Advisor.
- A course may be withdrawn from only once. A second withdrawal from the same
  course is not permitted; the student must complete it.

## Procedure

Withdrawal is requested through the student portal under Academic Services. The
request is routed to the Academic Advisor for approval, then to the Registrar
for processing. **A withdrawal is irreversible once processed by the Registrar**
— the course cannot be reinstated in the same term.

Students holding a scholarship should consult Financial Aid before withdrawing,
as dropping below 12 credit hours may suspend scholarship payments for the term.
