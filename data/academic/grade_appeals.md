# Grade Appeals

A student who believes a final grade was calculated or recorded incorrectly may
file a formal grade appeal.

## Deadline

Appeals must be submitted within **15 calendar days** of the date the grade was
published on the student portal. Appeals received after this deadline are not
considered, regardless of merit.

## Procedure

1. Discuss the grade with the course instructor first. Most discrepancies are
   resolved at this stage and no appeal is needed.
2. If unresolved, complete **Form AR-12 (Grade Review Request)** on the student
   portal under Academic Services.
3. The Department Head reviews the appeal and responds within 10 working days.
4. If the student remains dissatisfied, a final appeal may be made to the
   College Academic Committee within 7 days of the Department Head's decision.

## Grounds

An appeal must state one of the following grounds: a calculation or recording
error, work that was submitted but not marked, or a deviation from the
assessment scheme published in the course syllabus. Disagreement with the
academic judgement of the marker is not by itself a ground for appeal.
