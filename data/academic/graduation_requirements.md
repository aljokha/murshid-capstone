# Graduation Requirements

To graduate with a Bachelor's degree from Al-Noor University a student must
satisfy all of the following:

1. Complete a minimum of **132 credit hours**, distributed as:
   - 30 credit hours of University Requirements
   - 24 credit hours of College Requirements
   - 66 credit hours of Major Requirements
   - 12 credit hours of Electives
2. Achieve a cumulative GPA of **2.00 or above**.
3. Pass the Senior Capstone Project (6 credit hours, included in the Major
   Requirements above).
4. Complete a minimum of **8 weeks of supervised field training**.
5. Clear all financial, library, and laboratory holds.

## Time limits

The maximum period for completing the degree is **8 years** from first
registration. Approved leaves of absence are excluded from this count.

## Applying to graduate

Students must submit a graduation application through the student portal in the
term in which they expect to complete their final requirements. The deadline is
the end of week 4 of that term. Late applications defer graduation to the
following term, even if all academic requirements are met.

A typical full-time student completes 15 credit hours per term and finishes in
approximately 9 terms.
