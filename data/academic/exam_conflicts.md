# Examination Conflicts and Deferred Examinations

## Scheduling conflicts

A conflict exists when a student is scheduled to sit two final examinations in
the same time slot, or three final examinations on the same calendar day.

Conflicts must be reported to the Examinations Unit within **3 working days** of
the final examination timetable being published. The Examinations Unit will
reschedule one of the affected examinations. Conflicts reported after this
window may not be accommodated.

## Deferred examinations

A student who misses a final examination for a documented medical or
compassionate reason may apply for a deferred examination. The application and
supporting documentation must reach the Registrar within **5 working days** of
the missed examination.

If approved, the grade is recorded as **IN** (incomplete) until the deferred
examination is sat. Deferred examinations are held in the first two weeks of
the following term. An IN grade that is not resolved within that window
converts automatically to an F.

## Conduct

Academic misconduct in an examination — including unauthorised materials,
communication with another candidate, or impersonation — is referred to the
College Disciplinary Committee and may result in a grade of F for the course,
suspension, or dismissal.
