# Academic Probation and Dismissal

## Placement on probation

A student whose cumulative GPA falls below **2.00** at the end of any term is
placed on academic probation for the following term.

## Conditions while on probation

- Maximum registration load is reduced to 12 credit hours.
- The student must meet their Academic Advisor at least twice during the term.
- The student may not hold an elected student union position.

## Clearing probation

Probation is cleared when the cumulative GPA returns to 2.00 or above at the
end of the term.

## Dismissal

A student who remains below a cumulative GPA of 2.00 for **two consecutive
terms** on probation is subject to academic dismissal from the programme.

A dismissed student may petition the College Academic Committee for
reinstatement after one full term has elapsed. Reinstatement petitions must
include a written study plan endorsed by an Academic Advisor.

Summer term does not count as one of the two consecutive terms for dismissal
purposes, but a summer term may be used to raise the cumulative GPA and clear
probation.
