# Clubs and Societies

The Student Activities Office in **Building 2, room 1-110** oversees all
registered student clubs. Open Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

## Joining a club

Registered clubs recruit at the Activities Fair in week 2 of each term, and
accept members throughout the year through the student portal under Student
Life > Clubs. There is no membership fee for most clubs; those with equipment
costs (diving, motorsport, robotics) charge a subscription set annually.

## Starting a new club

To register a new club you need:

1. A minimum of **15 founding members**, all currently registered students.
2. A written constitution stating the club's purpose, how officers are elected,
   and how funds are handled.
3. A **faculty advisor** who is a full-time member of academic staff.
4. Approval from the Student Activities Office, which reviews applications
   monthly.

Registered clubs may apply for an annual budget of up to 8,000 SAR, book
university rooms free of charge, and use the university name in their title.

## Events

Any club event held on campus requires an event permit submitted at least **10
working days** in advance. Events involving external speakers, off-campus
travel, or expenditure above 3,000 SAR require additional approval from the Dean
of Student Affairs.

Clubs that hold no events and file no annual report for two consecutive terms
are de-registered.
