# Student Portal — Login and Password

The student portal is the single sign-on system for registration, grades,
timetables, transcript requests, and service forms.

## Resetting a forgotten password

1. Go to the portal login page and select "Forgot password".
2. Enter your university ID number and your registered mobile number.
3. A one-time code is sent by SMS. The code expires after 10 minutes.
4. Enter the code and set a new password.

New passwords must be at least 10 characters and include an uppercase letter, a
number, and a symbol. A password may not be reused within 12 months.

## Account lockout

The account locks after **5 consecutive failed login attempts**. The lock
releases automatically after **30 minutes**, or immediately if you visit the IT
Helpdesk in person with your student card.

## Registered mobile number out of date

If the SMS never arrives, your registered mobile number is probably out of
date. It cannot be changed online. Visit the IT Helpdesk in Building 4 with your
student card, or email helpdesk@alnoor.edu.example from your university email
address if you can still access it.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is required for all students from the start of the
term. Enrol a device under Portal Settings > Security. Students who have not
enrolled a device by the end of week 2 lose portal access until they visit the
IT Helpdesk.
