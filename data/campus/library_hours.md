# Central Library — Hours and Access

## Opening hours

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| Sunday - Thursday, normal term | 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM |
| Saturday, normal term | 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM |
| Friday | Closed |
| **Finals week** | 8:00 AM - **2:00 AM** (Sunday - Thursday) |
| Semester break | 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Sunday - Thursday |

During finals week the library extends closing to 2:00 AM and opens the second
and third floor study halls for silent individual study. Free tea and coffee are
available on the ground floor from 9:00 PM.

## Access

Entry is by student card. Cards are scanned at the turnstile. A lost card must
be replaced at the Student Services desk in Building 2 before library access is
restored.

## Borrowing

- Undergraduate students: 6 items, 14 days, renewable twice
- Postgraduate students: 12 items, 30 days, renewable twice
- Reference and reserve items: library use only, not borrowable

Overdue items accrue a fine of 2 SAR per item per day, capped at the
replacement cost of the item. An unpaid library fine places a hold on the
student's record and blocks transcript requests.
