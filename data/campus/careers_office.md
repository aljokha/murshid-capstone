# Careers and Employability Office

Located in **Building 2, first floor, room 1-104**. Open Sunday to Thursday,
9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Appointments are booked on the student portal under Student
Life > Careers.

## Services

- **CV and cover letter review** — 30-minute appointment, feedback within the
  session. Drop-in reviews are available every Tuesday, 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM, with
  no appointment needed.
- **Mock interviews** — 45-minute session with a recorded playback, bookable up
  to twice per term.
- **Internship placement** — the office maintains agreements with partner
  employers and matches students to summer placements. Applications for summer
  internships open in week 3 of the spring term and close at the end of week 8.
- **Field training coordination** — the office signs off the 8-week supervised
  field training required for graduation and liaises with the host employer.

## Career fair

The annual Career Fair is held in week 7 of the autumn term in the Main Hall,
Building 1. Over 60 employers attend. Registration is free but required, and
students must bring a printed CV. The office runs CV clinics in the two weeks
before the fair.

## Graduate outcomes

The office surveys graduates six months after graduation and publishes an annual
employability report on the university website.
