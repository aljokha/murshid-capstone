# Student Wellbeing and Counselling

The Student Wellbeing Centre is located in **Building 2, ground floor, room
G-018**, with a separate discreet entrance from the courtyard side.

## Counselling

Individual counselling is **free of charge** to all registered students. Each
student may book up to **6 sessions per academic year**; further sessions are
arranged case by case with the Centre's clinical lead.

Sessions are **confidential**. Information is not shared with academic staff,
family members, or sponsors without the student's written consent, except where
there is an immediate risk to life, which is the only statutory exception.

Appointments are booked by email at wellbeing@alnoor.edu.example or in person.
Typical waiting time is 3 to 5 working days.

## Same-day support

A duty counsellor is available without an appointment every day from 10:00 AM to
2:00 PM for students who need to speak to someone urgently. Outside these hours,
the campus Security Office in Building 1 can contact the on-call counsellor.

## Other support

- **Academic stress workshops** — group sessions before each examination period,
  covering revision planning, sleep, and exam anxiety.
- **Disability and learning support** — assessment for examination adjustments
  such as extra time, a separate room, or a reader. Requests must reach the
  Centre at least 4 weeks before the examination period.
- **Peer support scheme** — trained student volunteers, matched by college.
