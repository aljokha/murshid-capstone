# Campus Dining

## Locations

| Outlet | Building | Hours (Sun - Thu) |
|---|---|---|
| Main Dining Hall | Building 3 | 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM |
| Library Cafe | Central Library, ground floor | 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM |
| Engineering Cafeteria | Building 7 | 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM |
| Residence Kitchen Grill | Student Residences | 6:00 PM - 11:00 PM |

All food served on campus is halal certified.

## Meal plans

| Plan | Meals per week | Cost per term |
|---|---|---|
| Full | 19 | 3,800 SAR |
| Standard | 14 | 2,900 SAR |
| Light | 7 | 1,600 SAR |

Meal plans are charged to the student card and are mandatory for students living
in on-campus housing (minimum: Standard). Plans may be upgraded at any time and
downgraded only during the first two weeks of the term.

## Dietary requirements

The Main Dining Hall offers vegetarian and gluten-free options at every service.
Students with a documented allergy should register with Dining Services at
dining@alnoor.edu.example so that kitchen staff are notified; a dedicated
allergen-free preparation area is available on request.
