# IT Helpdesk

## Location and hours

The IT Helpdesk is located in **Building 4, ground floor, room G-012**, next to
the main lecture theatre entrance.

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Sunday - Thursday | 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM |
| Friday | Closed |

- Telephone: internal extension **4400**
- Email: helpdesk@alnoor.edu.example
- Ticket system: available on the student portal under IT Services

## What the helpdesk handles

- Student portal and email account problems
- Campus WiFi (network name `AlNoor-Student`) connection issues
- Printing quota and print release problems
- Software licences for students (statistical, design, and engineering packages)
- Laptop loans for students whose device is under repair (48-hour loan, subject
  to availability)

## What the helpdesk does not handle

Personal device hardware repair, personal software purchases, and academic
questions about course software — the latter should go to the course
instructor.

## Response times

Password and access issues are handled at the desk immediately. Logged tickets
are acknowledged within 4 working hours and resolved within 2 working days for
standard requests.
