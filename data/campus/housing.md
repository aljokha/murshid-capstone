# Student Housing

## Application

Applications for on-campus housing open in week 6 of the preceding term and
close at the end of week 10. Applications are submitted on the student portal
under Student Life > Housing.

Places are allocated by the following priority order:

1. Students whose registered home address is more than 150 km from campus
2. Students on full academic scholarship
3. First-year students
4. All other applicants, by application date

## Fees

| Room type | Fee per term |
|---|---|
| Shared twin | 4,500 SAR |
| Single | 7,200 SAR |
| Single with private bathroom | 9,000 SAR |

Fees are payable in full before move-in. A refundable deposit of 1,000 SAR is
charged against damage and is returned within 30 days of check-out following a
room inspection.

## Rules

- Quiet hours are 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM.
- Guests must be signed in at the residence desk and may not stay overnight.
- Cooking appliances are permitted only in the shared kitchens on each floor.
- Smoking is prohibited throughout the residences.

Withdrawal from housing mid-term forfeits the term's fee. Students leaving
campus housing must complete a check-out inspection to recover the deposit.
